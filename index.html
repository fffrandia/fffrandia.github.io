I. Proclamation of Sovereignty

We, the people of the Neutral Principality of Frandia, on the historic grounds of Kolodeznaya in the Voronezh Oblast, founded by our forefather in 1947, do hereby proclaim our sovereign, independent, and eternal existence.

Frandia is not a rebellion. Frandia is not a joke. Frandia is the restoration of historical justice, the memory of our ancestors, and the embodiment of our right to self-determination.

"Built on ashes. Restored by will. 1947."

II. The Sovereign

The Head of State is the Prince. The Prince is the Supreme Commander of the Armed Forces, the Guarantor of the Constitution, and the highest judicial authority. Power is transferred by hereditary right through the direct bloodline.

The current reigning monarch is His Serene Highness, Gleb I, Prince of Frandia, First of His Name.

III. Diplomatic Statement

Frandia seeks peaceful coexistence, cultural exchange, and official recognition from the United Nations, BRICS, the European Union, and all sovereign states of the world.

We are a peaceful nation. We are a neutral territory. We are ready for dialogue.
