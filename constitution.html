CONSTITUTION

of the Neutral Principality of Frandia

HOME CONSTITUTION NEWS

We, the citizens of the Neutral Principality of Frandia, founded in 1947 on the historic lands of Kolodeznaya, honoring the memory of the first settlers, having survived fire and rebirth, do proclaim this Constitution as the supreme law of our land.

Chapter I. Foundations of the State

Article 1.

The Neutral Principality of Frandia is a sovereign, neutral, and independent state. The official state language is English. The national currency is the Kholodin.

Article 2.

The territory of Frandia is one, indivisible, and sacred. The borders follow the historically established fence. No change of borders is permitted without the consent of the Prince and a referendum of all citizens.

Article 3.

Frandia proclaims permanent neutrality. It is forbidden to join military alliances, to host foreign troops, or to produce weapons. All disputes shall be resolved peacefully.

Chapter II. Citizens

Article 4.

Citizens of Frandia are: humans, cats, and dogs born on the territory of the Principality; and persons who have obtained citizenship in accordance with the migration law.

Article 5.

All citizens are equal before the law, regardless of species, sex, or age.

Article 6.

A foreigner may obtain citizenship after: 30 days of residence on the territory of the Principality; obtaining a Residence Permit; approval by the State Duma and the Government; and final confirmation by the Prince.

Chapter III. The Prince

Article 7.

The Head of State is the Prince. Power is transferred by heredity through the direct bloodline. The Prince is the Guarantor of the Constitution, the Supreme Commander-in-Chief, and the highest judicial instance.

Article 8.

Should the Prince be underage, a Regency Council is appointed. The Chair of the Council shall be the Mother-Founder of the state.

Chapter IV. Parliament

Article 9.

Legislative power consists of two chambers: the Government (Upper Chamber) — ministers appointed by the Prince; and the State Duma (Lower Chamber) — representatives of the citizens.

Article 10.

A law is adopted upon approval by both chambers and the signature of the Prince.

Chapter V. Armed Forces

Article 11.

The Armed Forces of Frandia consist of three dogs: a German Spitz, a Pomeranian Spitz, and a mixed Spitz. Their sole task is the defense of the borders. Attack is forbidden by the status of neutrality.

Chapter VI. Symbols

Article 12.

The state symbols are: the Flag, the Coat of Arms, and the Anthem. Their descriptions shall be approved by separate Decrees of the Prince.

Chapter VII. Final Provisions

Article 13.

The Constitution is the supreme law. No act may contradict it.

Article 14.

The Constitution enters into force upon signature by the Prince.

Signed: His Serene Highness, Gleb I, Prince of Frandia.
